Shomer is protecting you

שומר means “guardian”. Four layers of protection are now active on every site you visit.

One more step

Your browser asks you to approve access to websites before Shomer can block ads and trackers on them.

🧱 Ads & trackers blocked

EasyList, EasyPrivacy, uBlock filters and Peter Lowe’s list, compiled into fast native browser rules.

🧠 New trackers learned

Third parties that follow you across three sites are blocked automatically, even if no list knows them yet.

🎭 Fingerprinting defeated

Canvas, WebGL and audio readouts get invisible noise that changes every visit.

🍪 Cookie banners rejected

Shomer clicks “reject” for you where it can, and hides the rest. It never clicks “accept”.

Use the toolbar icon to see what was blocked on a page or to turn Shomer off for one site. Everything else is in the dashboard. Shomer collects no data.
