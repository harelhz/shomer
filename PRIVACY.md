# Privacy policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

**Shomer does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.**

- **No telemetry, no analytics, no accounts.** Shomer makes no network requests of its own. Its filter lists are bundled with the extension and updated only when the extension is updated.
- **What stays on your device.** Shomer stores, in your browser's extension storage, only what it needs to work: your settings and filters, the per-site choices you make, the third-party domains it has learned to block together with the sites they were seen tracking you on, and simple counters (for example, how many requests were blocked). Per-tab statistics are kept in session storage and discarded when the browser closes.
- **What it reads.** To block ads and trackers, Shomer observes the requests your browser makes and reads the pages you visit. This happens entirely inside your browser and is never recorded beyond the local data described above.
- **Removing your data.** _Dashboard → Trackers → Forget learned trackers_ erases learned data; uninstalling the extension erases everything.
- **Signals it sends on your behalf.** Shomer adds the Global Privacy Control (`Sec-GPC: 1`) and Do Not Track (`DNT: 1`) headers to your requests, which you can turn off in the dashboard.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/harelhz/shomer/issues.
